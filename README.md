Bold Penguin provides a digital exchange platform for commercial insurance, leveraging its DeX AI technology to connect carriers, brokers, and agents. The company offers APIs for submission ingestion, market intelligence, and marketplace access, enabling automated quoting, binding, and data-driven insights across the insurance value chain. Its solutions aim to modernize risk placement and improve efficiency for insurers and distribution partners.

**Areas:** Company · Insurance · Platform · Commercial · Artificial Intelligence

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Bold Penguin: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
